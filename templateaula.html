What would you do?

During the meeting, the sales director offered to extend the payment terms by 30 days if the client agreed to sign a two-year contract instead of one.

Why did the sales director offer to extend the payment terms?

1

Tap to reveal

To close a shorter, one-year deal quickly.

2

Tap to reveal

To secure a longer, two-year contract with the client.

3

Tap to reveal

To reduce the company's cash flow risk.

4

Tap to reveal

To match a competitor's lower price.

Grammar

Second Conditional — hypothetical situations

Affirmative If the client asked for a discount, we would review the terms.
Negative If the client didn't confirm today, we wouldn't hold the price.
Question If we offered a better price, would you sign today?

If + past simple, ... would + base verb — for imaginary or unlikely situations, not things happening now.
